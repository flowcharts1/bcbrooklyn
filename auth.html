Authenticator

Begin Typing

You must go to cuny mfa, login, and create a new mobile authentication factor. Copy the secret key and paste it below. Then, enter a shortcode, which you will use to access this key, such as "twt" or "hgfa" or "12e" any thing you'll remember. Upon reload, entering your correct shortcode will give you your TOTP based on the key you entered, you may set this new code as your default when you login. This system is designed to thwart the extra security layer that CUNY has mandated for accounts. Your secret key will be published to a database and be visible to anyone. It will be impossible to tell what account it is associated with however, unless you use an obvious shortcode with your name. Do this at your own risk.
